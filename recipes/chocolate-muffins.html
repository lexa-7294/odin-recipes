Chocolate Muffins

This recipe will make the best ever muffins that you ever tried!

This easy and quick recipe will make delicious muffins, perfect for busy mornings.

Ingredients:

  • flour
  • butter
  • eggs
  • sugar
  • vanilla extract
  • cocoa

Steps:

  1. Combine melted and cooled butter with sugar, eggs and vanilla extract.
  2. Add flour and cocoa and mix gentle.
  3. Bake at 160 degree Celsius for 45 minutes.
  4. Allow (if you can) to cool down a bit before enjoing the best ever muffins!
